Privacy and cookies

Audience measurement, with your permission

This portfolio uses Google Analytics 4 only if you accept audience measurement cookies. It helps Mathis Benchikh understand which pages and projects interest visitors, and measure clicks on CV and report links. A click does not prove that a file was saved or read.

Statistics may include pages viewed, visit dates and duration, traffic sources, approximate country and device type. A browser identifier helps estimate visitor numbers and return visits; it does not automatically reveal your name, LinkedIn profile or employer.

Accept, decline or change your mind

No Google Analytics tag is loaded before you agree. You can continue browsing all pages if you decline. The “Accept” and “Decline” buttons are presented equally. You can change your choice using “Cookie choices” at the bottom of the navigation.

Audience measurement is not enabled.

Retention and data processing

Your acceptance or refusal is saved in your browser for 180 days. Analytics cookies are configured to last no more than 180 days, without automatically extending their lifetime at each visit. If you withdraw permission, tracking stops and its cookies are deleted from this browser.

Google processes audience measurement data to produce reports for the site owner. Data retention depends on the Google Analytics property settings. Advertising features are disabled in this integration. Google’s processing may involve international transfers; see its privacy policy and information about partner sites.
