Research Internship — Trajectory Optimisation for Autonomous Drones

Three-month research internship in a high-level international environment, in collaboration with PIMM Laboratory in Paris. Research into trajectory optimisation and collision avoidance, with applications to autonomous drone navigation and guidance.

Co-author of a research paper

I contributed to writing a paper presenting the methods developed and their experimental validation. Submitted to the Journal of Control, Automation and Electrical Systems and currently under review.

  • Co-developed a variational planner based on the Euler–Lagrange equations and a reactive collision-avoidance corrector as part of a team, addressing fixed and moving obstacles.
  • Integrated the Python perception–guidance–control pipeline, with closed-loop trajectory tracking at approximately 20 Hz, validated on an instrumented rover platform.
  • Collaborative experimental validation: 40/40 goals reached without collisions across two repeated scenarios; simulation-to-test comparison and analysis of accuracy, safety margins and tracking robustness.
