Student Wellbeing Association Founding team · 2026

Turning a shared need into a dedicated space

Conversations with students and surveys highlighted a shared need on campus: a quiet place to pause, decompress, or rest between classes, especially during the lunch break.

As part of the founding team, I helped establish the association and took part in negotiations with the university administration, as well as managing and maintaining the space. The initiative led to a relaxation room opening at Arts et Métiers in April 2026. It taught me how to turn a shared need into a practical outcome through coordination and day-to-day follow-through.
