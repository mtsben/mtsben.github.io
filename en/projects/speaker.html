Piezo Speaker

A stereo speaker concept in which two basalt-fibre composite wings become the vibrating surfaces.

01 · PURPOSE

Turn a composite structure into a sound source

The goal was to design, manufacture and test a small stereo piezoelectric speaker. Surface-bonded PZT actuators excite the two wings, while their opening angle offers a simple way to explore sound directivity.
