Aerospace Engineering

I’m Mathis BENCHIKH, a graduate engineering student pursuing a double degree at Arts et Métiers and Cranfield University, where I am studying for the MSc Aerospace Vehicle Design. Fascinated by flight and exploration since childhood, I am developing a versatile profile in mechanical design, numerical simulation, and guidance and control. My studies combine a broad engineering foundation with a specialisation in aerospace vehicles.

My team projects connect modelling and experimental validation, from aircraft brake sizing to trajectory optimisation research with applications to autonomous drones. During my research internship at CNRS@CREATE in Singapore, I worked on planning, collision avoidance, and trajectory tracking in a high-level international research environment, comparing simulations with experiments on rovers.

I am seeking an aerospace master’s thesis internship lasting 4 to 6 months and starting in May 2027 with a major aerospace company. I would like to contribute to ambitious industrial R&D projects while deepening my expertise in aerospace engineering.
