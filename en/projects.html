// INTERNATIONAL RESEARCH · SINGAPORE

Trajectory Optimisation for Autonomous Drones

Three-month research internship at CNRS@CREATE in Singapore (June–August 2026), in collaboration with PIMM Laboratory in Paris, within a high-level international research environment. Collaborative development of trajectory optimisation and collision-avoidance methods, with applications to autonomous drone guidance. The perception–guidance–control pipeline was experimentally validated on rovers by comparing simulations with physical tests.

I contributed to writing a research paper as a co-author, presenting the methods developed and their experimental validation. The paper has been submitted to the Journal of Control, Automation and Electrical Systems and is currently under review.

AMSPACE C’Space · CNES & Planète Sciences

Participation in the CNES C’Space Rocket Competition

C’Space is a national student space-project event organised by CNES and Planète Sciences. The challenge is to design, build and test a rocket that carries scientific experiments and returns under parachute, while meeting safety and technical qualification requirements. With AMSPACE, the Arts et Métiers student astronautics association, we developed a carbon-fibre mini-rocket to take part.

Head of Theory and Integration

I coordinated theory and integration work with the other divisions, connecting simulations, onboard experiments and the vehicle’s technical requirements. My goal was to ensure that these elements worked coherently together before the rocket was presented at the competition.

Onboard Experiments
Designed and implemented experiments with the instrumentation team to study in-flight pressure, velocity and loads, with the aim of comparing models against future measurements.
Aerodynamic Simulation
Carried out CFD simulations as a team to analyse flow around the rocket and pressure distribution, supporting aerodynamic design decisions.
Integration and Qualification
Coordinated the integration of experiments and subsystems, performed technical checks and prepared the qualification evidence required before presenting the project at C’Space.
